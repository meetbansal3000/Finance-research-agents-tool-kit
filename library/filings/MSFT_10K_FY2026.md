## Item 1. Business

Microsoft is a technology company whose mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more. We develop and support software, services, devices, and solutions that deliver new value for customers and help them realize their full potential in an increasingly digital and AI-driven world.

Our operations are structured into three reportable segments:
1. **Productivity and Business Processes:** Includes Microsoft 365 Commercial and Consumer (Office 365, SharePoint, Exchange, Microsoft Teams), LinkedIn professional network, and Dynamics business solutions (Dynamics 365 ERP and CRM).
2. **Intelligent Cloud:** Consists of public, private, and hybrid server products and cloud services, notably Azure, Windows Server, SQL Server, Visual Studio, and GitHub.
3. **More Personal Computing:** Encompasses Windows OS OEM licensing and commercial products, Surface devices and accessories, Gaming (Xbox hardware, Xbox content and services, and Activision Blizzard game franchises), and Search and News advertising.

## Item 1A. Risk Factors

Our operations and financial condition are subject to material risks and uncertainties:

1. **Intense Competition and Technology Disruption:** We face fierce competition across all segments, including cloud hyperscalers (Amazon Web Services, Google Cloud) and enterprise productivity providers. The rapid pace of artificial intelligence (AI) development requires significant continuous capital investment in datacenter capacity, custom silicon, and foundation models. If we fail to anticipate customer adoption patterns or generate sufficient commercial returns on our accelerated AI infrastructure investments, our operating margins and return on invested capital could be adversely affected.
2. **Significant Capital Expenditure Commitments:** Capital expenditures have increased materially due to unprecedented investments in cloud computing and AI infrastructure, including leased datacenters, high-performance computing clusters, networking infrastructure, and energy procurement. There can be no assurance that these large-scale capital deployments will yield commensurate revenue growth.
3. **Regulatory Scrutiny and Antitrust Compliance:** We are subject to intense regulatory oversight, antitrust investigations, and digital market regulations across the United States, the European Union (Digital Markets Act), the United Kingdom, and other jurisdictions regarding cloud licensing, generative AI partnerships, and software bundling. Adverse regulatory rulings could mandate operational changes, fines, or structural remedies.
4. **Cybersecurity and Service Availability:** As a provider of mission-critical cloud infrastructure and software to governments and enterprise corporations globally, sophisticated cyberattacks, service outages, or data breaches could harm our reputation, expose us to substantial legal liability, and disrupt customer operations.

## Item 7. Management's Discussion and Analysis of Financial Condition and Results of Operations

### Fiscal Year 2026 Financial Highlights
- **Revenue:** Revenue was $331,839 million for fiscal year 2026, compared to $281,724 million in fiscal year 2025, an increase of 17.8%, driven by strong momentum across Intelligent Cloud and Productivity and Business Processes.
- **Operating Income:** Operating income increased 20.8% to $155,237 million, compared to $128,528 million in the prior fiscal year. Operating margin expanded to 46.8%.
- **Net Income:** Net income grew 31.3% to $133,749 million, compared to $101,832 million in fiscal year 2025. Diluted earnings per share increased to $17.95.

### Liquidity and Capital Resources
Cash provided by operating activities was $182,935 million for fiscal year 2026, compared to $136,162 million in fiscal year 2025, driven by higher operating earnings and disciplined working capital management.

Capital expenditures, including payments for property, plant, and equipment, were $115,948 million in fiscal year 2026, compared to $64,551 million in fiscal year 2025 and $44,477 million in fiscal year 2024. The substantial acceleration in capital expenditures reflects strategic expansion of cloud and AI datacenters and high-performance server hardware globally.

Free cash flow (operating cash flow less capital expenditures) was $66,987 million in fiscal year 2026, down from $71,611 million in fiscal year 2025, as growth in operating cash flow was offset by record infrastructure spending.

As of June 30, 2026, total cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were $76,843 million (comprising $20,935 million in cash and cash equivalents and $55,908 million in short-term investments). Total debt obligations were $40,294 million (current portion of $9,227 million and long-term debt of $31,067 million), leaving a net cash position of $36,549 million.

Working capital metrics showed trade accounts receivable of $80,876 million as of June 30, 2026, compared to $69,905 million as of June 30, 2025 (growth of 15.7%, trailing revenue growth of 17.8%). Inventories stood at $1,397 million compared to $938 million in the prior year.
