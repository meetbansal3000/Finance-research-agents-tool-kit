# Tata Consultancy Services Limited - Audited Financial Results (FY2024-25)
**Company Name:** Tata Consultancy Services Limited (TCS)  
**Reporting Currency:** INR (₹ Crores)  
**Period Ended:** March 31, 2025  
**Document Type:** Audited Consolidated Financial Results / Press Release  
**Accounting Standard:** Ind AS (IFRS-converged)  
**Source URL:** https://www.tcs.com/content/dam/tcs/investor-relations/financial-statements/2024-25/q4/press-release.pdf  

---

## Page 1 (FY25 Headline Highlights)
Operating Margin: 24.3%; expanded 20 bps YoY.
Company-reported Free Cash Flow for FY 25 was ₹46,449 crore, representing 95.7% of net profit.

---

## Page 4 (Consolidated Audited Statement of Profit and Loss)
Revenue from operations for the year ended March 31, 2025 was ₹255,324 crore compared to ₹240,893 crore in the previous year, an increase of 6.0%.
Operating profit (EBIT) before other income for FY2025 stood at ₹62,074 crore (Operating Margin 24.31%). Total EBIT including operating adjustments stood at ₹62,293 crore (24.40%).
Profit for the year attributable to shareholders of the company: ₹48,553 crore (Total profit including non-controlling interest ₹244 crore is ₹48,797 crore).
Profit for FY2024 attributable to shareholders was ₹45,908 crore (Total profit ₹46,099 crore).

---

## Page 5 (Consolidated Balance Sheet)
Cash and cash equivalents (₹6,765 crore) + Other current investments / deposits (₹34,968 crore) = ₹41,733 crore.
Total borrowings and lease liabilities: ₹11,283 crore (Current borrowings ₹1,554 crore; Lease liabilities ₹9,729 crore).
Current borrowings due within one year: ₹1,554 crore.
Trade receivables: ₹45,510 crore (vs ₹43,550 crore in FY24, +4.50% YoY).
Trade receivables prior year: ₹43,550 crore.

---

## Page 6 (Consolidated Statement of Cash Flows)
Cash generated from operating activities: ₹48,908 crore.
Purchase of property, plant and equipment and intangible assets: ₹3,937 crore.
Free Cash Flow derived as Operating Cash Flow (₹48,908 crore) minus Capital Expenditures (₹3,937 crore) = ₹44,971 crore.
Net Cash position: Total Debt (₹11,283 crore) - Cash & Investments (₹41,733 crore) = -₹30,450 crore.
